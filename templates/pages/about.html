Why this exists

Factories, stations, shops, farms and homes produce hours of audio that nobody can listen to continuously. Important moments — a machine starting to fail, glass breaking at night, someone calling for help — are easy to miss or are noticed too late.

SonicSentinel does the continuous listening, flags the few seconds that matter, and leaves the final judgement to a person.

What it recognises

Ten mandatory sound classes, each with a default severity. Administrators can change the rules that turn a detection into an alert.

Two independent models

Every recording is classified twice. A custom Python model (a convolutional network trained on log-mel spectrograms) and a Google Teachable Machine audio model each look at the same audio window without seeing the other's answer.

When they agree, the result is more trustworthy. When they disagree, the recording goes to a reviewer instead of raising an alarm on a guess.

Who uses it

Normal userUploads recordings and follows their own history.
Audio reviewerListens to uncertain events and confirms or corrects them.
Security operatorAcknowledges, escalates or dismisses security alerts.
Maintenance operatorFollows up machinery fault alerts.
AdministratorSets thresholds and alert rules, sees every event.

Privacy and responsibility

The microphone starts only after the browser asks for permission and shows a clear "live" state. Live windows are classified and deleted; only uploaded recordings are stored, and only their owner and administrators can open them.

Limits

SonicSentinel is a competition prototype for controlled, ethical testing. It is not a certified emergency-response or law-enforcement system. Accuracy depends on the training data, microphone and background noise, so every critical result can be reviewed by a person.

Built with

Flask, MySQL or SQLite, librosa, TensorFlow/Keras and scikit-learn for the Python model, Google Teachable Machine for the second model, and plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript for the interface. Built for the TechWiz 7 NextWave AI and ML category (theme: AcousticX Intelligence).
